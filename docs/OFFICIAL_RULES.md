# Official rules and interpretation

Source: `2026-INTRAMURAL-PROPOSAL-FINAL-1.pdf` (49 pages). PDF pages match printed pages. The original and the two supplied rule-table images are preserved in the repository; they are not public web assets.

## Three independent levels

1. Match outcomes determine tournament progress, never official college points.
2. Athletics/Arnis/Taekwondo final discipline/division results contribute internal sub-points separately for Men and Women (p.13).
3. Reviewed championship standings award general points once per category (pp.3–4). The public overall tally sums only these published awards.

Athletics individual sub-points are 5/4/3/1; relay sub-points are 10/8/6/2. Arnis and Taekwondo division sub-points are 5/4/3/1. Two eligible athletes from one college can both contribute. No points for unplayed events (p.19). Enter participation eligibility explicitly; absence is not participation. Ambiguous disciplinary eligibility requires an official ruling.

Organizer-confirmed: CODM plus MLBB championships = 40; Dance Sports Latin, Standard and Third Kind championships = 24; Bandurria plus Classical Guitar championships = 10. There is no extra generic E-Games award. All sport categories are Men/Women except Sepak Takraw (Men only).

## Decisions requiring organizer confirmation

- Cheerdance is offered (pp.28–29) but absent from the general-points table: block general awards until a rule is entered with an official source.
- Pageant allows two male and two female candidates per college (p.32). Aggregation is not specified; general awards stay blocked until policy is confirmed. Results can still be approved and published.
- Athletics relay labels on p.13 appear as “400m x100m” and “400m x400m”. Proposed normalized labels 4x100m/4x400m remain inactive until confirmed. Long Jump (p.13) and Triple Jump (p.18) are TBA and inactive.
- Taekwondo specific rules say single elimination whereas the general sports section says double elimination. Staff enter committee-approved fixtures; no automatic bracket generation.
- MLBB group descriptions and playoff formats conflict (pp.33–34). Staff select explicit stages/series lengths. For declared BO3 league stages, 2–0 yields 3/0 internal league points; 2–1 yields 2/1. These never enter the college tally.
- CODM approved round-robin results use wins, map difference, maps won, then head-to-head / tied mini-table (pp.39–40). Any remaining tie needs the prescribed official ruling. Double forfeits record two losses and 0–2 maps each (p.37).
- Championship ties require an official playoff/ruling (p.13). Overall equal point totals share rank; no invented medal tiebreaker.
- Program dates and weigh-in times conflict. No published fixtures are generated from the proposal.

Any administrator may approve corrections, including their own. Keep reasons, before/after history and the previous public revision until approval. Rule changes must not silently rewrite existing published awards.
